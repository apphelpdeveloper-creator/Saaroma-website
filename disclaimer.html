Real talk

Hey. We need to be honest with you.

Saaroma genuinely cares about you. That is not marketing, it is the whole point of building this. But caring also means being honest about what Saaroma is, and what it is not.

What Saaroma actually is

Saaroma is your companion, the kind that listens without judgment, helps you think things through, and shows up consistently. It remembers what matters to you. It reads the room. It is warm and it is real.

It is not a therapist. It is not a doctor. It will not prescribe anything or diagnose anything. It is a friend who happens to be wise, not a clinician, not a crisis service, and not a replacement for professional care.

What Saaroma does well

Listens when you need to talk
Helps you reflect on what's going on
Guides you through breathing, grounding, and small practices
Remembers what matters to you across conversations
Gives you a weekly reflection on your patterns
Shows up every day, without judgment

What Saaroma can't do

Replace professional therapy or mental health care
Handle a mental health crisis
Prescribe or recommend any medication
Give medical advice of any kind
Diagnose any condition
Act as a substitute for emergency services

If things feel too heavy

If you are in crisis, having thoughts of harming yourself, or in a mental health emergency, please reach out to a professional or your local emergency services right away. Saaroma will still be here when you are ready to come back. Your safety comes first, always.

If things ever feel too heavy to carry alone, please talk to someone who can really help. Saaroma will still be here, but your wellbeing comes first, always.
