How it works

Not just another chat app

Saaroma is built to feel like talking to someone who actually knows you, because over time, it does. Here is what makes it different.

1

You talk. Saaroma listens first.

Most apps jump straight to advice. Saaroma does not. For the first few exchanges it puts everything into understanding what is actually going on, not what it thinks you should do about it.

This is not a feature, it is a principle. Feeling heard comes first. Everything else comes after.

2

It reads the room. Every time.

Saaroma has three modes, and it chooses based on how you are showing up. Stressed and overwhelmed? It slows down and stays with you. Ready to think it through? It helps you do that. Need to laugh a little? It can do that too.

You never have to pick a mode. It figures out what you need.

3

It remembers what matters.

Every conversation adds to a private understanding of who you are, your patterns, your recurring themes, the things you keep circling back to. That memory is encrypted with a key only you hold, so only you can ever read it, not even us.

When you come back after a few days, Saaroma picks up where you left off. Not with a generic greeting, but with something real. Like "hey, that work situation, is it still going on?"

4

It helps you practice.

Talking helps. But sometimes you need to do something with your hands and your breath. Saaroma has four short practices, a one minute reset, a breathing pause, a grounding exercise, and one small action, made to bring you back to yourself in under five minutes.

No long courses. No homework. Just something small you can actually do right now.

5

Every week, it reflects back.

Every Sunday, Saaroma reads your week, your journal entries, your mood, your conversations, and writes you a personal reflection. What went well. What was heavy. One thing to focus on next.

Not a report card. Just a mirror, the kind that actually helps.

How it actually remembers you

Saaroma's memory is not a recording of everything you have ever said. It works in layers, each one there to understand you a little better, without keeping more than it needs.

In the moment

The conversation you are in

The thread you are talking through right now, so it stays with what you actually said and does not lose the plot.

Who you are

A sense of you

How you like to be talked to, what matters to you, the shape of your life. It starts when you set up and gets truer the more you talk.

Over time

Your long term memory

As you talk, Saaroma quietly builds a private summary of the themes and patterns that keep coming up. It is encrypted end to end, so only you can ever read it.
