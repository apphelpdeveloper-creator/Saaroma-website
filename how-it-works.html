How it works

Not just another chat app

Saaroma is designed to feel like a conversation with someone who actually knows you — because over time, it does. Here's what makes it different.

1

You talk. Saaroma listens first.

Most apps jump straight to advice. Saaroma doesn't. For the first few exchanges, it focuses entirely on understanding what's actually going on — not what it thinks you should do about it.

This isn't a feature. It's a principle. Feeling heard is the first step. Everything else comes after.

2

It reads the room. Every time.

Saaroma has three modes — and it chooses based on how you're showing up. Stressed and overwhelmed? It slows down and stays present. Ready to think it through? It helps you do that. Need to laugh a little? It can do that too.

You don't have to tell it which mode to use. It figures it out.

3

It remembers what matters.

Every conversation adds to a private understanding of who you are — your patterns, your recurring themes, the things you keep coming back to. Stored only on your device, never shared.

When you come back after a few days, Saaroma picks up where you left off. Not with a generic greeting — with something real. Like "hey — that work situation, is it still going on?"

4

It helps you practice.

Talking helps. But sometimes you need to do something. Saaroma includes four short practices — a one-minute reset, a breathing pause, a grounding exercise, and a one small action prompt — designed to bring you back to yourself in under five minutes.

No long courses. No homework. Just something small you can actually do right now.

5

Every week, it reflects back.

Every Sunday, Saaroma reads your week — your journal entries, your mood patterns, your conversations — and writes you a personal reflection. What went well. What was heavy. One thing to focus on next week.

Not a report card. Just a mirror. The kind that helps.

Three layers of memory

Saaroma's memory isn't a database of everything you've said. It's a layered system designed to understand you — without storing more than it needs to.

Layer 1

Working memory

The last 10 messages of the current conversation. What you're talking about right now.

Layer 2

Session memory

Your profile — who you are, how you like to communicate, what matters to you. Built during onboarding and refined over time.

Layer 3

Long-term memory

Every 20 messages, Saaroma generates a private summary of recurring themes, patterns, and progress. Stored only on your device.
