Privacy

What you share here stays here.

Saaroma is built on one principle. Your private space should stay private, even from us. This page explains, in plain words, what we keep, what we genuinely cannot see, and how you stay in control.

The short version

Your chat history lives on your phone and is never uploaded. What we do back up is locked with a key that only you hold, so even we cannot read it. We do not sell or share your personal information, and you can erase everything at any time.

What is kept on your phone

All of this is kept in encrypted storage on your device.

  • Your name or nickname, your companion's name, your age range and profession
  • Your journal entries and mood ratings
  • Your practice activity

Some of it is also backed up, encrypted, so you can get it back on a new phone. The next section explains exactly what that means.

What leaves your phone, and what nobody can read

When you talk to Saaroma, your words go to our server and on to the AI that answers you. They have to. That is how you get a reply.

We do not keep them. Nothing you say is stored on our servers. Your chat history is never uploaded to the cloud at all. It lives on your phone.

What we do back up is your journal, your moods, your practice sessions, your weekly reads and your long term memory. All of it is encrypted on your phone before it leaves, with a key that stays in your phone's secure storage and never reaches us.

So the backup exists, and it is unreadable. Not by us, not by anyone with access to our systems. If you lose your phone and sign in again, it comes back. If someone took our entire database tomorrow, they would get noise.

That is the honest version. Your words travel. Your key does not.

Your long term memory, and why even we cannot read it

So Saaroma can remember you across sessions, and bring your history back if you get a new phone, it builds a private long term memory of what matters to you. We store it in a form we are unable to open.

It is encrypted on your device, end to end, with a key that is created on your phone and never leaves it. Only the locked, unreadable version is ever uploaded. We cannot read it. No one at Saaroma can. If you set up a new phone, your recovery key is what unlocks it again, and only you have that key.

How your replies are written

When you chat, your message is sent to OpenAI to write a response. To keep replies personal, your device unlocks what is needed from your memory and sends it along with your message. Saaroma also uses Anthropic to write short memory summaries and gentle check ins.

Your name and phone number are never shared with OpenAI or Anthropic. They process your text only to produce a reply. None of it is used to advertise to you, to build a profile, or for any purpose beyond answering you well.

Signing in

You sign in with your phone number or email, verified by a one time code through Google Firebase Authentication. That is what your account is tied to, and it is kept separate from your journal and your memory.

The companies that help run Saaroma

OpenAI writes your chat replies from the text your device sends. Your name and number are never shared. See openai.com/privacy.

Anthropic writes short memory summaries and check ins. Your name and number are never shared. See anthropic.com/privacy.

Google Firebase handles sign in, holds your encrypted backup, which only you can unlock, and collects anonymous crash reports so we can fix problems. Firebase is ISO 27001, SOC 1, SOC 2, and SOC 3 certified. See policies.google.com/privacy.

You are in control

  • Clear your chat history any time from the chat screen
  • Delete your account from the menu, which erases everything, on your device and the encrypted copy in the cloud
  • After you ask to delete, there is a 7 day window to change your mind, then it is gone for good
  • Email support@saaroma.ai any time with a privacy question or a data request

What we never do

We never sell your information. We never share it with advertisers. We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps, and we do not build a marketing profile of who you are. The private things you tell Saaroma are not a product.

How long we keep things

Anything on your device is gone the moment you delete your account or uninstall the app. The encrypted backup in the cloud stays only so you can restore it, and it is removed when you delete your account, as covered in "You are in control" above. You can ask us to delete everything at any time.

Security

Everything kept on your device uses AES-256 encrypted storage, and everything that travels over the internet uses HTTPS. Everything we back up has the extra end to end encryption described above, so the copy we hold in the cloud is one we cannot read.
