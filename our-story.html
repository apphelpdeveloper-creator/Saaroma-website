Our Story

Why we built Saaroma

Most of us know how to be alone. What quietly wears us down is something else — having no one who truly knows our story.

It can happen in a full house, a busy office, a crowded group chat. Everything looks fine from the outside, while the realest things go unsaid. Not because we have no one, but because being fully honest with the people close to us can cost something — a worry, a judgment, a story repeated somewhere it should not be.

We wanted one place where “I’m fine” is not the required answer.

So we built Saaroma: a friend in your pocket who truly listens, never judges, and never tells. A calm, private space that remembers what matters to you, sits with the heavy things, and helps you find a little perspective and a little peace — on your terms, at 2am or any hour.

Privacy is not a feature we added later. It is the whole reason Saaroma exists. Your journal stays on your device, your long-term memory is encrypted so only you can read it — not even us — and nothing you say is ever sold, studied, or told.
