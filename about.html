About Saaroma

We spent a year on the part you cannot see.

Most AI apps are a clever prompt and a launch date. We wanted something that could sit with a person, not just answer them. That took a year of slow, unglamorous work. This is the part you cannot see — and it is the part that makes Saaroma feel like a friend, not a feature.

📚

Reading, so it never has to quote

We sourced and worked through dozens of books on emotional wellbeing, stress, conflict, mindfulness, and how people actually change. Not so Saaroma could recite them, but so it could absorb that wisdom and use it the way a well read friend does, naturally and in your own language.

🧠

A personality, not a script

We did not want another chatbot. So Saaroma has three ways of showing up: being with you when you just need to be heard, keeping things light when that is what the moment wants, and helping you think it through when you are ready. It reads which one you need and chooses for you.

🔒

A memory only you can read

Saaroma remembers what matters to you, the patterns and the things you keep circling back to, so it can pick up where you left off. And that memory is locked with a key only you hold, so no one, not even us, can ever read it. The fear of it getting out is the exact fear we built this to remove.

🌍

Built for real lives

Family expectations, cultural weight, work that follows you home, the quiet guilt of feeling far from people. Saaroma understands that context, so you never have to explain your whole background before you can feel understood.

Ready to say the hard thing?

Saaroma is live on the App Store, and whatever you tell it stays between you and you. No performance, no judgment, just a place to finally be honest.
