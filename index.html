What Saaroma offers

Everything you need to feel heard

💬
A friend who listens
Talk to Saaroma like a warm, sharp friend. It listens first, asks the right questions, and never rushes you toward an answer you did not ask for.
📓
Somewhere to put it down
Write about your day and track how you feel. It saves as you type, and your journal stays on your device.
🧘
A minute to breathe
Four short sessions, a one minute reset, a breathing pause, grounding, and one small action, to bring you back to yourself.
✦
Your Score, every Sunday
Every Sunday, Saaroma reads your week and writes you a personal reflection. What went well, what was heavy, and one thing to focus on next.
🔒
Yours alone
Your long term memory is encrypted so only you can read it, not even us. No selling your information, no ads, and no profile built about you.
🌍
No backstory needed
Saaroma gets family pressure, career stress, and the weight that comes with different cultures, without you having to explain the backstory first.
🌱
A companion that grows with you
Saaroma is not a quick fix, it is a practice. Like any real relationship, it deepens over time. We recommend using Saaroma consistently for at least 6 months to feel its full benefit. The people who journal regularly and sit with the weekly reflection tend to find the most clarity and calm. The more you show up, the more Saaroma understands you.
Our promise

What you share here stays yours

Saaroma is built on one principle. The private things you say should stay private, even from us.

  • Your journal and mood notes stay on your device
  • Your long term memory is encrypted end to end, so only you can read it, not even us
  • To write a reply, your messages go to OpenAI and Anthropic. Your name and number are never shared with them.
  • We never sell or share your personal information, and there are no ads
  • Delete your account and everything goes, on your device and in the cloud

🛡️
Private by default
This is the whole reason Saaroma exists. The hardest things you carry should have somewhere safe to land, not somewhere they can be sold, studied, or leaked. So we built it that way from day one.
